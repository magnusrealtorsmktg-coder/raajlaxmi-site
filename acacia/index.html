      About project

      At Acacia, every day has its own rhythm. Residences, commercial spaces, and lifestyle amenities come together to make room for fitness, leisure, connection, and everything in between.

      A community where there’s always something to do, somewhere to unwind and more ways to enjoy the everyday.

      Ganpati Mandir

      A dedicated Ganpati Mandir within the complex adds a sense of community and familiarity.
